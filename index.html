About Us

EzBot is a passionate robotics team formed by former students of ISAE-ENSMA who have now become professional engineers. United by our shared educational journey and continued passion for innovation, we are dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what's possible with robotics technology. Our diverse engineering backgrounds and collaborative spirit drive us to create cutting-edge solutions that bridge the gap between academic excellence and real-world applications.
