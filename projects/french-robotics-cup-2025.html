French Robotics Cup 2025

Competing at the highest level of French robotics

About the Project

The French Robotics Cup is one of the most prestigious robotics competitions in France, bringing together the best teams from universities and engineering schools across the country. In 2025, instead of participating directly, we decided to help our school's team with their robot development while simultaneously demonstrating our own capabilities.

Our Robot

As a demonstration of our engineering skills, we designed and built a 3-wheeled holonomic robot in just one week. This project showcased our ability to rapidly develop both hardware and software solutions. Our robot features:

  • 3-wheeled holonomic drive system for improved maneuverability
  • Custom LiFePO4 battery pack for reliable power delivery
  • Reused mainboard from last year's robot for cost efficiency
  • Advanced computer vision for object recognition and navigation
  • Precision actuators for object manipulation
  • Real-time decision-making algorithms
  • Robust mechanical design for reliability under competition conditions

Design Evolution

This year's robot represents a significant evolution from our previous 4-wheeled holonomic design. We moved to a 3-wheeled configuration to solve the persistent problem of getting hung up on non-planar surfaces that plagued our previous robot. The 3-wheeled design provides:

  • Better ground clearance and obstacle negotiation
  • Improved stability on uneven surfaces
  • Simplified mechanical design while maintaining holonomic capabilities
  • Reduced weight and complexity compared to 4-wheeled systems

Project Goals

Rather than competing directly, our goals for this project were to:

  • Demonstrate rapid prototyping capabilities (1-week build)
  • Support our school's official competition team
  • Validate the 3-wheeled holonomic design concept
  • Test integration of reused components with new designs
  • Showcase the quality of ISAE-ENSMA engineering education
  • Push the boundaries of our technical capabilities within tight constraints
